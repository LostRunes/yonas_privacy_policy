Yonas is a community app for finding and sharing street-food stalls, campus spots and restaurants. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why it is collected, who it is shared with, and the choices you have over it.

In this policy, “we”, “us” and “the developer” mean Abinash Mohanty, the independent developer of Yonas. “You” means the person using the app.

What Yonas does not do

Yonas contains no advertising and no advertising identifiers. It uses no third-party analytics, tracking or profiling SDKs. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers. We never collect your contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone audio or browsing history.

02 Location data in detail

Location is requested only while the app is in use, and only after you grant permission at the operating-system prompt. Yonas does not collect location in the background and does not track your movement over time.

Location is used in two ways:

  • To sort places by distance from where you are. This happens on your device and the coordinate is not stored on our servers.
  • To position a place you add. When you add a stall, spot or restaurant to the map, the coordinate you choose is stored and shown publicly as that place’s location.

You can refuse the permission or revoke it at any time in Android Settings › Apps › Yonas › Permissions. The app remains usable without it; you will need to search or place pins manually.

03 Camera and photos

Yonas requests camera and photo-library access only at the moment you choose to add an image. We access only the specific images you select. We do not scan, index or upload your photo library.

Photos you submit are uploaded to our image hosting provider (see section 5) and become part of the public content attached to the place, review or post you added them to.

Before you upload

Photographs may contain embedded metadata, including the location where they were taken. Consider this before uploading an image taken somewhere private.

04 Content that is public

Yonas is a community app, and much of it is public by design. The following are visible to anyone using the app, including people who are not signed in:

  • Your display name, username, profile picture and bio
  • Any stall, spot, restaurant, review, rating, reply, post or story you publish
  • Photographs you attach to that content
  • Your position on community leaderboards
  • Whether you were recently active in the app

Please do not post anything you would not want publicly associated with you. Content you publish may be seen, screenshotted or copied by others, and we cannot recall those copies.

06 Where your data is held

Our providers operate data centres in several countries, so your data may be stored or processed outside the country you live in, including in the United States and the European Union. Providers are selected on the basis that they offer appropriate contractual safeguards for international transfers.

07 How long we keep it

  • Account data is kept while your account exists.
  • Content you publish is kept until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Backups may retain deleted data for up to 30 days before being overwritten.

08 Your rights and your choices

You may at any time:

  • Access the data held about you
  • Correct your profile, directly in the app
  • Delete individual items of content you created
  • Delete your account and all data attached to it
  • Withdraw permissions for location, camera and photos in Android Settings
  • Object to our processing, or request a portable copy of your data

Depending on where you live, these rights may be granted to you by law — for example the GDPR in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, or the DPDP Act in India. We honour these requests regardless of where you live.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Yonas account from Settings › Delete account inside the app. You may also request deletion by emailing us at the address below from the address associated with your account.

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, your email address, your uploaded photographs and the content you created. We complete deletion requests within 30 days. Aggregate counts that cannot identify you may remain.

09 Children

Yonas is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

10 Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Access to stored data is restricted by row-level security rules so that a signed-in user can modify only their own records. Upload credentials are short-lived and issued only to signed-in users; no private key is stored in the app.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify affected users and any regulator we are required to inform.

11 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The revision date at the top always reflects the current version. Where a change materially affects how we handle your data, we will give notice in the app before it takes effect.
